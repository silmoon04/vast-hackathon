What this analysis measures

This deploys Pinch Point’s occupancy method on the platform’s YOLO11 detection sidecars. Counts are the rounded median of frame detections in each second. They measure objects visible together, not unique identities. Gaps stay unknown. Captions help select footage; answers are computed from detections.
